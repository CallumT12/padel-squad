🔒 Deletion PIN

When set, this PIN must be entered before removing records or sessions in Match History and before deleting Calendar weeks.

📦 Archives

Store a named snapshot of the current session that can be opened in a separate read-only browser tab to review past performance and compare changes.

🗓️ Reset calendar

Deletes the Matches/Calendar weeks in the chosen month (or every week with "All months"), including availability, lineups, reserves, entered results and Americano scores. Submitted results stay in Match History and the leaderboard. Players, fee and payments are kept. A backup is saved first – if it fails, nothing is deleted.

🗄️ Backups

A full backup (scores, history, calendar, fee, payments) is saved automatically once a week when anyone opens the site. Only the latest 3 backups are kept – older ones are deleted automatically.
Back up now saves a backup online (counts towards the 3 kept).
